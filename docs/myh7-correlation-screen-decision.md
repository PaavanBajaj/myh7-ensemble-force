# MYH7 correlation screen decision, 2026-10-02

## Decision

The next focused question is whether the positive rank relationship between actin-activated `kcat_rel` and unloaded `v_rel` survives **matched-preparation, common-assay measurement** across a short-head MYH7 variant panel. The [new protocol](matched-atpase-velocity-protocol.md) specifies the panel, measurements, and interpretation rule. This is a measurement-validation question, not a claim that ATPase kinetics directly determine sliding speed or a choice of a molecular mechanism.

The structural PPS/IHM comparison of D778V, L781P, and S782N remains an option after better comparable measurements. The present screen does not select it: those three velocity ratios share Morck slide/control contexts, and the WT-state structural annotations cannot estimate mutant PPS or IHM populations.

## Evidence considered

The [audited data dictionary](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data-dictionary.md) and [cohort ledger](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data/velocity-cohort-ledger-v1.csv) define the study population. There are 17 frozen `kcat_rel` values, 16 public canonical within-study `Na_rel` values, and 15 Table 1-aligned unloaded `v_rel` values from nine studies. Three additional human HCM velocity pairs (I467V, R671C, M493I) are sensitivity leads only; eight Table 1 variants lack typed matched absolute speeds. The Morck source workbook contributes 32 selected slide/channel ratios across five variants, not 32 independent variants or biological preparations.

The [comparison plan](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data/comparison-plan-manifest-v1.json) was locked before estimates. All 78 planned pairs and their variant/study-labeled [plots](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data/plots/C001.svg) are in the [result table](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data/comparison-results-v1.csv). The primary method is tie-aware Spearman rho on **ratios**, except binary-feature group contrasts. These values describe a small selected collection of heterogeneous experiments; no p-values, confidence intervals, or multiplicity-adjusted discoveries are claimed.

- `kcat_rel` versus `v_rel`: rho **+0.670**, 14 paired variants. Its leave-one-study descriptive range is **+0.588 to +0.888**. Excluding the five Morck velocity variants gives rho **+0.717**, 9 variants. A short-head, pure-actin MVEL20 subset has only four points, so its rho **+0.800** is especially fragile. The association is a reason to standardize and remeasure, not confirmation of a universal mechanochemical law. Several individual variants are discordant, notably E497D (`kcat_rel` 1.516, `v_rel` 0.808).
- `Na_rel` versus `v_rel`: rho **−0.570**, 13 paired variants; same-source subset rho **−0.790**, 11. Assay and construct differences, proxy status, and shared publication contexts prevent mechanistic interpretation.
- `kcat_rel` versus `Na_rel`: rho **−0.548**, 14 paired variants. The [dependence ledger](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/data/dependence-ledger-v1.csv) identifies seven that reuse exact mutant short-head ATPase `S`; excluding them gives rho **−0.487**, n=7. Also excluding three risky public `Na_rel` rows leaves rho **−0.632**, n=4. Neither tiny sensitivity subset resolves the algebraic/selection problem. Where the same WT short-head and long-head components are paired, `Na_rel = L_rel / S_rel` and `kcat_rel = S_rel`; the negative relationship can be partly constructed by the denominator. Morck WT denominator identity is less certain than the shared mutant `S` identity.

Five literature-selected, label-blind WT structural features were generated before associations. For `v_rel`, their rank rhos span **−0.455 to +0.350**, with only 12–15 paired sites per comparison. ADP-pocket distance (`rho −0.192`), ELC distance (`+0.139`), charge (`−0.069`), and RSA (`−0.099`) are weak in this cohort. No feature is a measured mutant perturbation. The new ADP-pocket distance and the existing catalytic-motif distance themselves correlate strongly (`rho +0.932`, n=15), so related geometry must not be counted as independent support. The binary IHM flag has only one exposed group member in the velocity comparison; its median contrast is marked fragile, not a stable correlation. Full weak, null, and larger effects remain visible in the unfiltered results.

## Source and uncertainty limits

The primary velocity ratios preserve absolute WT/mutant speeds and source metrics. Y115H gives 406/903 ≈0.450 (**−55%**) rather than the compiled −45%; P710R gives 239/762 ≈0.314 (**−69%**) rather than −50%; I457T gives 1700/700 ≈2.429 (**+143% change**), while Table 1's 240 approximates mutant speed as a percent of WT. R663H and several Morck variants were described as unchanged; their small nonzero ratios are measured estimates, not established effects. R403Q's selected MVEL20 was chosen to match Table 1 from several same-condition metrics, and alternate metrics are retained in sensitivity output.

Some ratios mix sS1 and 2-hep HMM, MVEL/MVEL20/MVIS/filtered MVEL/`v0`, temperatures and conditions. D239N/H251N and Y115H/E497D share printed WT velocity values. Morck WT workbook rows 81 and 88 recur across all five variants. Filament-level fits, slides, and repeated temperature/metric versions are not independent biological studies. Source SEM/SD values, where typed, are retained; ratio error propagation ignores unknown WT/mutant covariance and is descriptive. G768R error type is unspecified. M493I's reported SD describes filament distribution and was not propagated to a ratio uncertainty.

The static 8ACT OFF-state and 8EFD/8EFE/8EFI actomyosin structures give site geometry, not mutant conformational state, kinetics, or a predictor. Distal variants are absent from some active-state templates. The actin complex includes tropomyosin and porcine actin, unlike the pure-actin motility label. The five features and their coverage are documented in the [selection review](../workflows/myh7-correlation-screen/feature-literature.md).

## Other reasonable follow-ups

After standardized paired measurements, a focused ADP-detachment or step-size experiment could investigate the observed velocity heterogeneity, using direct kinetic or optical-trap endpoints. A structural PPS/IHM comparison could follow if variants with matched phenotypes and sufficient preparation quality justify it. Recovering numeric absolutes for the eight unresolved Table 1 rows would also improve the observational screen. None is implied by the current rank associations.
