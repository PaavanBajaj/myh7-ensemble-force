# Velocity structural-feature literature review

**Reviewed and frozen before outcome-feature calculation:** 2026-10-02 20:35 UTC. This note uses primary experimental work and deposited structures. A geometric value at a WT site is a structural annotation; it is not a measured mutant effect or a velocity predictor.

Unloaded filament speed is jointly affected by effective displacement per interaction and the kinetics of actomyosin detachment; the common approximation `v ≈ step size / attached time` depends on assay regime and motor density. [Liu et al. 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6092189/) and [Vander Roest et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8214707/) provide relevant primary measurements. [Doran et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1085/jgp.202213267) resolved human cardiac actomyosin with and without ADP and reports nucleotide-pocket rearrangement coupled to relay, converter, and an approximately 16° lever swing. [Morck et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.76805) measured distinct motility and step-size behavior for pliant and light-chain-binding MYH7 variants. These experiments motivate geometric annotations for the measured unloaded assay; none establishes that any one WT distance predicts a mutation's effect.

## Candidate ranking, fixed before associations

Ranking weighs prior experimental link to actomyosin speed or its kinetic/step-size determinants, specificity to the unloaded assay, reproducibility from deposited coordinates, canonical-site coverage, and computational cost. All five selected concepts are newly generated numeric features. The existing 8ACT RSA and IHM-contact features remain separate comparators.

| Rank | Concept | Choice and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ADP-to-rigor WT-site displacement | **Selected.** Directly tracks geometry along the ADP-release-associated movement resolved by Doran; distal lever sites are missing. |
| 2 | ADP-pocket proximity | **Selected.** Nucleotide residence is tied to detachment, and 8EFE has a deposited ADP ligand; proximity alone is not a release rate. |
| 3 | Rigor actin proximity | **Selected.** Represents WT location relative to the strong-binding interface; the deposited filament has tropomyosin and porcine actin, unlike the pure-actin velocity assays. |
| 4 | Relay mechanical-pathway proximity | **Selected.** The relay/converter linkage moves during product release. A prespecified landmark set in the complete human 8ACT structure provides full-site coverage. |
| 5 | Essential-light-chain proximity | **Selected.** The light-chain-supported lever is relevant to step size and the Morck variants; the 8ACT folded-back state provides complete coverage, but is an OFF-state snapshot. |
| 6 | Local Cα contact density | Not selected: reproducible, but a less velocity-specific proxy for packing; related to existing RSA. |
| 7 | 8ACT WT-site RSA | Existing, clearly defined comparator rather than a newly discovered velocity feature; OFF-state accessibility is a weak surrogate for unloaded motility. |
| 8 | Pliant-region class / Pro-in-helix flag | Retained as descriptive context. Sparse binary/categorical contrasts would align strongly with the single Morck study, and Pro identity is sequence-based. |
| 9 | 8ACT IHM-partner distance/contact | Existing regulatory comparator, closer to head availability than short-head unloaded speed. |
| 10 | Mutant FoldX score or pilot geometry | No validated values or authorized production mutant-structure workflow. |

Human WT pre-power-stroke [9I8P](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9I8P) was considered, but its observed MYH7 coordinates end at 780 and it is MgADP·VO4-bound. It is not silently substituted for missing ADP/rigor sites. Bovine 6FSA/8QYU and the G768R preparation pilot are not used as feature values.

All sites use the one-based human MYH7 [P12883](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P12883/entry) sequence. For these pinned structures, author residue numbers are the canonical positions; the generator checks every observed MYH7 residue letter against P12883 at that number and records both author and label sequence IDs. This is an explicit number-and-identity mapping check, not a de novo sequence alignment. Missing residues or required atoms yield missing values with reasons, never zero. Per-chain canonical-to-PDB author/label mappings are retained in the site audit. The manifest distinguishes the 8ACT gzip download checksum from its decompressed PDB input checksum. Related distances and the existing catalytic-motif proximity may correlate; the feature-to-feature screen addresses that dependence without counting them as independent mechanistic evidence.
