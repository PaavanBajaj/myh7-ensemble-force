# Data dictionary and comparison rules

This private, descriptive screen was frozen on 2026-10-02. [Source hashes](data/source-manifest-v1.json), [feature definitions](feature-literature.md), and [the pre-analysis plan](data/comparison-plan-manifest-v1.json) identify its inputs. A blank value means unavailable under the stated cohort rule, not zero.

## Units and cohorts

- `kcat_rel`: frozen mutant/within-study WT actin-activated ATPase `kcat` ratio, dimensionless. Its 17 canonical rows and measurement IDs come from `workflows/kcat-rel/data/` unchanged.
- `Na_rel`: public canonical `LSAR_mut / LSAR_WT_study`, dimensionless, 16 rows. LSAR is a proxy for head availability, not a direct head count or IHM occupancy. The separate [Na components](data/na-components-v1.csv) expose reported short-head `S` and long-head `L` ATPase values and WT context where recoverable.
- `v_rel`: mutant/within-study WT unloaded pure-actin gliding velocity, dimensionless. The primary cohort has 15 Table 1 variants from nine papers. A single variant estimate is used in across-variant comparisons. Three outside-Table-1 human MYH7 HCM leads have `cohort=expanded_sensitivity` and appear only in explicit velocity sensitivities. Eight Table 1 records without typed matched absolute WT/mutant speeds stay unresolved. See the [cohort ledger](data/velocity-cohort-ledger-v1.csv).
- `ln_value` in [outcomes](data/outcomes-v1.csv) is a transformation for secondary analysis. The primary correlations use the unlogged ratios. The optional `secondary_log_pearson` transforms outcome ratios with the natural logarithm and leaves structural features on their raw scales.

## Structural and reference features

The five new geometry columns in [features](data/features-v1.csv) are `adp_rigor_ca_shift_A`, `adp_adenine_ca_distance_8efe_A`, `rigor_actin_min_heavy_distance_8efi_A`, `relay_landmark_min_ca_distance_8act_mean_ab`, and `elc_min_heavy_distance_8act_mean_ab`. All are Ångström distances between deposited **WT** coordinates at the variant site and a specified reference or state. Exact atoms, alignment, chain mapping, and limits are in [feature-literature.md](feature-literature.md). They are not measured mutant changes. [Site mapping](data/site-mapping-v1.csv) and [coverage](data/feature-coverage-v1.csv) record observed residue IDs and missing reasons for every registry site.

Reference comparator columns in [analysis-table](data/analysis-table-v1.csv) are `delta_charge` (formal charge change, ordinal sequence descriptor), `grantham_distance` (sequence substitution score), `catalytic_motif_min_ca_distance_8act_mean_ab` (existing WT geometry, Å), `wt_site_rsa_8act_mean_ab` (existing WT relative solvent accessibility, fraction), and `ihm_interface_flag_8act` (existing binary WT contact annotation). `delta_charge` appears once even though both older feature tables carry it. The IHM flag has very small exposed group counts and is reported as a group contrast. Geometric features can be correlated descriptions of the same site and structural fold.

The [analysis table](data/analysis-table-v1.csv) is one row per variant in the 22-site union. `*_study` identifies the publication for each outcome. `v_rel_expanded` is populated only for the three separate sensitivity leads. `study_context` is a readable summary; it is not an independence key.

## Evidence and dependence fields

The [outcome table](data/outcomes-v1.csv) keeps `source_id`, DOI, locator, local row reference, `measurement_ids`, construct family/detail, assay/filament, metric, temperature, absolute WT/mutant values, source units and errors, WT context ID, and aggregation method. Velocity summary inputs and within-study alternatives are separate files. `table1_number` and `table1_agreement` record the compiled claim without replacing the primary absolute pair. Y115H, P710R, and I457T have documented magnitude/convention discrepancies.

`uncertainty` is descriptive. Most summary pair ratio errors are first-order propagation of the reported component SEMs, assuming independence because their covariance is unavailable. They are neither cross-study confidence intervals nor evidence of independent biological preparations. G768R source error type is unspecified, so no ratio error is propagated. M493I's reported SD is a fitted filament-velocity distribution, so its ratio uncertainty is blank. Morck's sample SD is across selected slide/channel ratios, not biological-preparation SEM. The 32 selected Morck pairs use 17 slide IDs and 24 WT workbook rows; rows 81 and 88 are reused for all five variants. [Velocity dependence](data/velocity-dependence-v1.csv) names shared controls and alternate conditions.

The [kcat–Na dependence ledger](data/dependence-ledger-v1.csv) distinguishes same variant, same publication, exact reused short-head `S`, uncertain WT identity, and risk flags. Seven of the 14 overlapping variants reuse the same mutant short-head ATPase measurement in both outcomes. If their WT short-head component is also shared, `Na_rel = L_rel / S_rel`; a negative `kcat_rel`–`Na_rel` association can then arise algebraically. The seven are R249Q, I457T, D778V, L781P, S782N, A797T, and F834L. The Morck WT pairing differs in the public pooled LSAR and frozen paired ATPase table, so the exact denominator relation is uncertain there.

## Comparison artifacts

[Comparison inventory](data/comparison-inventory-v1.csv) was created before association estimates. It has 3 outcome–outcome, 30 outcome–feature, and 45 feature–feature pairs. `n_paired`, `paired_variants`, each outcome's study composition, and missing variants are explicit. [Results](data/comparison-results-v1.csv) preserve every pair, including weak effects; [points](data/comparison-points-v1.csv) give each plotted variant, value, and study. Each pair has a labeled [SVG plot](data/plots/C001.svg) through `C078.svg`.

For two nonbinary variables the primary estimate is tie-aware Spearman rank rho, descriptive only. A binary feature yields `median(flag=1) - median(flag=0)` in the other variable's units, with group sizes; an effect with fewer than two in either group is labeled fragile. `loo_variant_*` and `loo_study_*` are the min/max descriptive estimates after excluding one variant or all rows bearing one source study. Study deletion is not applicable to feature–feature pairs. [Sensitivity results](data/sensitivity-results-v1.csv) include same-source pairs, exact-S and risky-Na exclusions, a four-variant strict short-head MVEL20 velocity subset, Morck removal, expanded human velocity leads, and same-study alternative metrics/conditions. `n_paired` always counts variants, not experiments or slide records.

No exploratory p-values were computed, so there is no multiplicity-adjusted claim. These 78 overlapping comparisons are not independent tests. Leave-one-out ranges do not quantify population uncertainty. Assay, construct, study, and WT sharing prevent causal or predictive interpretation.
